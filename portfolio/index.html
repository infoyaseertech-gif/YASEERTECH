About me

Muhammad Yasir Jafar

Web developer · Founder of YaseerTech

I build websites and business systems that help Nigerian businesses look credible, work faster and stay organised.

I’m a web developer and the founder of YaseerTech. I take a project all the way from the first conversation to a live, working product: I design it, build it, set up the database and put it online, then stay close to improve it as your business grows.

The work in this portfolio is real. For AJIGS CONNECT I built a company website with a staff system behind it for jobs, clients, invoices and expenses. For Kabomo Collections and IHSAN Footwears & Poultry I built apps that owners run from their phones, replacing notebooks with stock, sales, receipts and reports they can trust.

For X and O, a Kaduna modest-wear brand, I built an online shop where stock is held the moment an order is placed, so two customers never buy the same last piece, plus a dashboard that shows the owner orders, stock and profit.

And for companies that need to be taken seriously, including Bluejay Structures, Maleektech Services, Marafa Multi-Services and Dan Musa Group, I build clear, professional websites that explain what the business does, show its credentials and make it easy to ask for a quote. Where a team needs a system, I add staff logins so each person sees only what their job allows.

What I care about most is solving the real problem. Before I write any code I ask how you sell, who needs to see what, and where your time is going. Then I build only what helps, explain it in plain language, and you deal with me directly, with no layers in between.

My background in meteorology and climatology, from my degree at FUT Minna, my placement at the Nigerian Meteorological Agency and my master’s studies, gave me a careful, data-minded way of working. I bring that habit into every system I build.

If your business needs a website, an online shop or a system to run it, let’s talk about it.
